A little about me

I'm a recent B.Tech graduate in Computer Science (Data Science) who's now taking their first real steps into building things with code. Coming from a Data Science background gave me a good foundation in logic and problem-solving, but building actual apps — with real users, real UI, real bugs — is a whole new world I'm genuinely excited to explore. My first proper project is an engineering notes website("Notesportal") built with HTML/CSS/JavaScript and Django, letting students log in, browse notes by branch and semester, with an admin panel to manage it all. Coming from a data-heavy academic background to hands-on app development has been a fun shift, and I like that every project teaches me something I didn't know I needed. I'm still early in this journey, but I show up curious, ready to break things, fix them, and figure out how it all works — one project at a time.
